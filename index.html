How it works

1

COLLECTION

Women receive the kit at a Health Center, and perform the self-collection privately with clear and safe instructions.

2

ANALYSIS

The device with the sample is returned to the healthcare staff. Partner laboratories analyse the samples.

3

RESULTS

Women receive their secure digital results along with personalised care recommendations.
